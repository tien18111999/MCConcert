More Than Just Music — A Celebration of Community

Welcome to the Community Music & Family Festival, hosted by Midwestern Career College. Designed to bring together 500 to 700 neighbors in a safe, welcoming, and vibrant space, this one-day festival blends local artistry, delicious food, and family-friendly entertainment.

Because this event is powered by our community, every dollar raised through donations, sponsorships, and vendor partnerships goes directly toward supporting our community fund's mission and keeping future neighborhood events thriving.

Live Community Stage

Enjoy 8 hours of continuous entertainment featuring 4 talented local bands/musicians and an energizing live DJ.

Local Food Court

Grab a bite from 5–6 favorite local food vendors serving up delicious meals, snacks, and drinks—supporting our community fund.

Interactive Kids Zone

Keep the little ones smiling all afternoon with face painting, balloon artists, arts & crafts, and safe lawn games.

Glow & Groove Finale

Closing out from 7:00 PM to 8:00 PM with an upbeat DJ finale, LED glow lights, and a massive community dance party!
